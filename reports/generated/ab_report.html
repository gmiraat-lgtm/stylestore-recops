What this experiment is

Two recommendation models serve the storefront simultaneously: the production champion and a challenger candidate. Every user is deterministically assigned to one variant, so each shopper consistently sees one model's recommendations. An impression is one rendering of the "Recommended for you" shelf; a click is a shopper opening a recommended product. Click-through rate (clicks / impressions) measures which model produces recommendations shoppers actually want - a live judgement that stays valid even when offline metrics become incomparable under data drift.

Reading and decision

On current data the champion shows the higher click-through rate. However, with fewer than 100 impressions per variant this difference is anecdotal, not statistically meaningful - the correct operational decision is to keep the experiment running and let more traffic accumulate before promoting either model.
