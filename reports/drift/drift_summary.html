What was checked, in plain terms

A recommendation model is only as good as its understanding of current customer behaviour. This check compared 30,000 interaction events from the newest week of store traffic against 30,000 events the current model was trained on. If customers now browse different categories, price ranges or sections than before, the model's suggestions gradually become irrelevant - this is called data drift.

What changed and what did not

The change score measures how different the new week looks from the training data (0 = identical, higher = more different). Scores above 0.1 read as a noticeable shift.
